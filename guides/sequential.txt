HOW TO PLAY — SEQUENTIAL MYSTERY
=================================

GOAL
----
Work your way through a series of clues, one at a time. Each clue
gives you information (and sometimes a puzzle to solve) that produces
an access code. Enter that code to unlock the next clue. Unlock all
the clues, then solve the final mystery.


STARTING THE GAME
-----------------
Enter the start code you were given to open the case. The game begins
with your first clue (or an introduction clue, if the mystery has one).


PROGRESS BAR
------------
The bar at the top of the page shows how many clues you've unlocked
out of the total (e.g. "3 / 8 clues"). Use it to track how far along
you are. A timer in the corner tracks how long you've been
investigating.


READING CLUES
-------------
Each clue is displayed as a card on the page. Read it carefully —
the information inside will help you figure out the access code to
unlock the next clue. New clues animate in when they're revealed.


ACCESS CODES — UNLOCKING NEW CLUES
-----------------------------------
At the bottom of the page (below your current clues) you'll see a
locked gate with a text box. This is where you enter the access code
to move forward.

  1. Study the current clue (and any associated evidence) carefully.
  2. Figure out the code from the clue's information or puzzle.
  3. Type the code into the box and tap UNLOCK (or press Enter).
  4. If correct, the next clue is revealed.
  5. If wrong, you'll see an error — double-check your work and try again.

  Codes are not case-sensitive. Spaces are usually ignored.


NEED A HINT?
------------
Stuck on figuring out a code? Look below the code-entry box for the
"💡 Need a hint?" button.

  • Tap it to REVEAL a hint for the current step.
  • Tap it again to HIDE the hint.

Hints are optional — try to solve it on your own first!


LOCKED CLUES
------------
Clues you haven't reached yet appear as grey locked indicators (🔒)
below your current gate. You'll unlock them one by one as you progress.


CLUE PAGES — EXTRA EVIDENCE
----------------------------
Some clues include a link to a special page with more details — this
could be an email inbox, a text conversation, an invoice, a receipt,
a map, a calendar, an access log, or another type of document.

  • Tap the link card on the clue (e.g. "View Email" or "View Map")
    to open it in a new tab.
  • These pages contain extra clues and details that help you crack
    the access code.
  • Return to the main game tab when you're done reading.

  On mobile, these pages open in the same tab — use the
  "← Back to Mystery" link at the top to return.


IMAGES — ENLARGE FOR A CLOSER LOOK
------------------------------------
Images attached to clues have an "⤢ Enlarge" button below them.

  • Tap "⤢ Enlarge" to open the image full-screen.
  • Tap outside the image (or the X) to close it.

Use this whenever an image contains small text or details you need
to read carefully.


PUZZLES
-------
Some clues include a puzzle to solve. Tap "🧩 Solve Puzzle" on the
clue to open it. Solving the puzzle reveals a reward or a piece of
information you need to continue.

On desktop, puzzles open in a new tab. On mobile, they open in the
same tab — tap "← Back to Mystery" to return after solving.

PUZZLE TYPES:

  DRAG AND DROP (Put in Order)
  ■ Items are listed in a scrambled order.
  ■ Drag them into the correct sequence.
  ■ On mobile, press and hold an item then drag it up or down.
  ■ Tap Submit when you're happy with the order.

  ELIMINATION (Cross Out)
  ■ A list of options is shown. Cross out the ones that DON'T belong
    based on the clues — leave the correct ones untouched.
  ■ Tap an item to cross it out (it turns red with a strikethrough).
  ■ Tap it again to bring it back.
  ■ Cross out exactly the number of items the puzzle specifies, then
    tap Submit.

  MATCH (Connect Pairs)
  ■ Two columns of items are shown. Connect each item on the left
    to its matching item on the right.
  ■ Tap an item on the left to select it (it highlights).
  ■ Then tap its match on the right — a line connects them.
  ■ To undo a pair, tap either connected item again.
  ■ Match all pairs, then tap Submit.

  WORD SEARCH
  ■ Find all the listed words hidden in the letter grid.
  ■ Drag across the letters to highlight a word (across, down,
    or diagonally).
  ■ Found words are crossed off the list and highlighted green.
  ■ Once all words are found, read the leftover letters top to bottom
    — they spell a hidden phrase that ends at ZZ.
  ■ Type that hidden phrase into the box and tap Submit.

  FILL IN THE BLANK
  ■ A passage of text is shown with blanks to fill in.
  ■ Easy mode: drag words from the word bank at the bottom up into
    the correct blanks, or tap a blank then tap a word to place it.
  ■ Hard mode: click a blank and type the answer directly.
  ■ Tap Submit when all blanks are filled.

  CIPHER / DECODE
  ■ An encoded message is shown.
  ■ Use the clue information to decode what it says.
  ■ Type your decoded answer into the box and tap Submit.

  PHONE KEYPAD
  ■ A code is shown using letters (like on a phone keypad: A=2, B=2, C=2).
  ■ Use the on-screen keypad to enter each number that matches the
    letters in the code, then tap Submit.

  ACCESS CODE (Straight Entry)
  ■ Use the clues to work out a numeric or word code.
  ■ Type it into the box and tap Submit.

  HOTSPOT (Click the Image)
  ■ An image is shown with hidden spots to find.
  ■ Click (or tap) each hidden location on the image to place a marker.
  ■ Green markers are close; red markers missed the spot.
  ■ Click a placed marker to remove it.
  ■ Find all spots, then tap Submit.


EVIDENCE LOCKER
---------------
Tap 🗂️ Evidence in the top bar to open the Evidence Locker. This
collects any bonus rewards or extra evidence you've unlocked by
solving clues and puzzles throughout the mystery. Review it at any
time for a summary of what you've gathered.


HOW TO PLAY BUTTON
------------------
Tap ❓ How to Play in the top bar at any time to re-read these
instructions.


RESET
-----
Tap ↺ Reset in the top bar to wipe ALL progress and start the
investigation from the very beginning.

  ⚠ This cannot be undone. All unlocked clues and puzzle solutions
    will be lost. Use it only if you want to start completely over.


FINISHING THE MYSTERY
---------------------
Once you've unlocked all the clues, a final gate appears. Enter the
solution code (or follow the final instructions) to reveal the answer
and close the case.


SAVING AND SHARING YOUR RESULT
-------------------------------
When the case is solved, a result card appears showing the mystery
title, your completion time, and whether you used any hints.

  • Tap "📷 Save/Share" to save or share the card image.

  On iPhone/iPad:
    The native share sheet opens. Choose "Save Image" to add it to
    your Photos, or pick any app (Messages, Instagram, etc.) to share
    it directly.

  On Android:
    The share sheet opens. Choose "Save to device" or pick an app
    to share it.

  On desktop:
    The image downloads automatically as a JPEG file. A share link
    is also copied to your clipboard if sharing isn't supported by
    your browser.

  • Long-press the card image on mobile to get the standard
    "Save Image" option directly from your browser.

The result card is yours to keep — share it with friends to show
off your detective skills!
