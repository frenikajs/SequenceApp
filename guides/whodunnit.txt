HOW TO PLAY — WHODUNNIT MYSTERY
================================

GOAL
----
You're a detective. Your job is to study all the evidence, narrow down
your list of suspects (and weapons, rooms, or other categories), then
make your Accusation at the bottom of the page. Get every category
right to close the case.


STARTING THE GAME
-----------------
Enter the start code you were given to unlock the case. Once entered,
the full investigation opens up.


CHARACTER CARDS
---------------
At the top of the page you'll see rows of cards — Suspects, Weapons,
Rooms, or whatever categories this mystery uses.

  • Tap any card image to ENLARGE it for a closer look.
  • Tap outside the enlarged image (or the X) to close it.


CASE FILES (Evidence)
---------------------
All the clues and evidence are listed below the character cards as
collapsible Case Files.

  • Tap a Case File header to EXPAND it and read the evidence inside.
  • Tap the header again to COLLAPSE it.
  • Work through all the files — every detail matters.

NEED A HINT?
  If you're stuck on a piece of evidence, look for the
  "💡 Need a hint?" button inside that Case File.
  • Tap it to REVEAL the hint.
  • Tap it again to HIDE it.


DETECTIVE'S NOTEPAD
-------------------
Tap the 📋 Notepad button (floating at the bottom of the screen) to
open your logic grid. Use it to track what you've ruled in or out.

HOW THE GRID WORKS:
  • Tap a cell ONCE     — marks ✗  (Ruled out / eliminated)
  • Tap a cell TWICE    — marks ●  (Confirmed / still in play)
  • Tap a cell THREE TIMES — clears the mark

Your notepad saves automatically in your browser — it will still be
there if you reload the page.

To wipe all your marks and start fresh, tap "Clear notepad" at the
bottom of the notepad panel.


MAKING YOUR ACCUSATION
----------------------
When you've studied all the evidence and worked the notepad, scroll
down to the ACCUSATION section.

  1. Use the dropdown for each category to choose your answer
     (e.g. Suspect, Weapon, Room).
  2. Tap "Submit Accusation" to lock it in.

READING THE RESULTS:
  ✓  (green checkmark) — that category is CORRECT. It locks in and
     you don't need to change it.
  ✗  (red X) — that category is WRONG. Update your pick and try again.

You can submit as many times as you need. Partial credit carries over
between attempts, so correct answers stay locked.


EVIDENCE LOCKER
---------------
Tap 🗂️ Evidence in the top bar to review any bonus evidence or rewards
you've unlocked from the case files.


HOW TO PLAY BUTTON
------------------
Tap ❓ How to Play in the top bar at any time to re-read these
instructions.


RESET
-----
Tap ↺ Reset in the top bar to wipe ALL progress and start the
investigation from the very beginning.

  ⚠ This cannot be undone. Use it only if you want to start over
    completely.


SAVING AND SHARING YOUR RESULT
-------------------------------
When the case is solved, a result card appears showing the mystery
title, your completion time, and whether you used any hints.

  • Tap "📷 Save/Share" to save or share the card image.

  On iPhone/iPad:
    The native share sheet opens. Choose "Save Image" to add it to
    your Photos, or pick any app (Messages, Instagram, etc.) to share
    it directly.

  On Android:
    The share sheet opens. Choose "Save to device" or pick an app
    to share it.

  On desktop:
    The image downloads automatically as a JPEG file. A share link
    is also copied to your clipboard if sharing isn't supported by
    your browser.

  • Long-press the card image on mobile to get the standard
    "Save Image" option directly from your browser.

The result card is yours to keep — share it with friends to show
off your detective skills!


TIPS FOR SOLVING
----------------
  • Open every Case File — don't skip any evidence.
  • Use the Notepad grid to cross off options as you eliminate them.
  • Mark ● (confirmed) when evidence points strongly to one option.
  • When all columns except one are crossed out (✗) in a row,
    that remaining option is your answer for that category.
  • Only submit your Accusation when you're confident — the ✓/✗
    feedback will tell you if any part is right.
