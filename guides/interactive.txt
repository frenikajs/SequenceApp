HOW TO PLAY — INTERACTIVE MYSTERY
==================================

GOAL
----
You and your team each step into the shoes of a different SUSPECT. As the
host unlocks each clue, you'll receive private information only your suspect
knows. Share what you learn, study the shared evidence together, and at the
end your whole team VOTES on who the real culprit is. Get the vote right to
solve the mystery!


THIS IS A GROUP GAME
--------------------
Interactive mysteries are played together as a group — there is no solo mode.
One person is the HOST (the lead detective who runs the game); everyone else
JOINS the host's investigation.


STARTING THE GAME
-----------------
HOST:
  1. Enter the start code you were given. You'll be taken straight to the
     lobby, where your GROUP CODE is displayed at the top.
  2. Read the group code aloud (or tap "Share Code") so your team can join.
  3. Wait until everyone has joined, then tap "Start Investigation".

EVERYONE ELSE:
  1. Enter the GROUP CODE from your host.
  2. You'll wait in the lobby until the host starts the mystery.

  ⚠ Once the host starts, the group is sealed — no one else can join.


CHOOSING YOUR SUSPECT
---------------------
After the host starts, everyone (including the host) picks a suspect.

  • Tap a suspect card to claim it.
  • Each detective must choose a DIFFERENT suspect — once someone takes a
    suspect, it shows as "Taken" for everyone else.
  • Not every suspect has to be chosen — only one per player.
  • A live counter shows "X of Y suspects assigned" so you know who's ready.

Once EVERY player has a suspect, the HOST's "Enter Crime Scene" button
unlocks. The host taps it to begin — and everyone is taken to the case.


PLAYING THE CASE
----------------
The case plays out one clue at a time, led by the host.

  • The host enters each clue's access code to unlock the next step for the
    whole team.
  • When a clue unlocks, everyone sees the shared clue — AND you get a
    POP-UP with YOUR suspect's private clue for that step. Only you see your
    suspect's clues.
  • Your suspect's name is shown in the top corner of the screen.

YOUR GAME CARD
  If your suspect has a game card, tap the "🎴 Game Card" button in the top
  bar to view it at any time (it may be an image or a PDF).

EVIDENCE LOCKER
  Tap "🗂️ Evidence" in the top bar to review everything you've gathered so
  far — your suspect's private clues as they unlock, plus any rewards earned
  from puzzles or clue pages.

NEED A HINT?
  If the host shares a hint for the current step, you'll see it appear on
  your screen.


THE GROUP VOTE
--------------
Once all the clues have been unlocked, it's time to accuse.

  1. Every player chooses the suspect they believe is the culprit and taps
     "Lock In Accusation".
  2. A counter shows "X of Y detectives have voted".
  3. When everyone has voted, the team's choice (the most-voted suspect) is
     revealed to all players.

IF THE GROUP IS RIGHT:
  🎉 "Correct! Your team solved the mystery." The solution is revealed along
  with the Case Closed card, the culprit, and a thank-you message.

IF THE GROUP IS WRONG:
  🚔 "The culprit got away!" The HOST then decides:
     • "Accuse Again" — the vote is cleared and the whole team votes again.
     • "Reveal Mystery" — the answer is shown to everyone. The Case Unsolved
       card appears with a "Better luck next time" message.


HOW TO PLAY BUTTON
------------------
Tap "❓ How to Play" in the top bar at any time to re-read these
instructions.


SAVING AND SHARING YOUR RESULT
-------------------------------
When the case ends, a result card appears showing the mystery title and your
team's time. Tap "📷 Save/Share" to save or share the card image — on mobile
the native share sheet opens (choose "Save Image" for Photos); on desktop the
image downloads automatically.


TIPS FOR SOLVING
----------------
  • Talk to your team! Your private suspect clues only matter if you share
    what they reveal.
  • Each suspect sees a different angle on the case — piece them together.
  • Don't lock in your vote until your team has compared notes.
  • The most-voted suspect becomes the team's accusation, so agree together
    before everyone votes.
